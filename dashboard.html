Guidelines & Blood Compatibility

Understand donation prerequisites and universal cross-matching.

Who Can Give Blood?

  • Age: 18 to 65 years.
  • Weight: Minimum 45 kg.
  • Health: Good general health.
  • Hemoglobin: Minimum 12.5 g/dL.
  • Not Eligible: Recent tattoos or specified surgeries.

Why Donate Blood?

Every two seconds, someone needs blood. Your single donation can save up to three lives.

"Tears of a mother cannot save her child. But your blood can."
